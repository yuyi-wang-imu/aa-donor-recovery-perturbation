# BMC Pharmacology and Toxicology submission alignment

The current manuscript is:

> Donor-aware transcriptomics and bidirectional Geneformer perturbation prioritize compound-associated targets in aplastic anemia

It is prepared as a regular **Research article** for **BMC Pharmacology and Toxicology**. The evidence order is database-compound provenance associated with herbs recurrent in published aplastic-anemia prescriptions, human hematopoietic context, a donor-aware healthy-directed longitudinal transcriptomic trajectory, bidirectional Geneformer prioritization, and downstream network/structural hypothesis generation. The database compounds were not chemically quantified or verified in an administered formulation. Eighteen compounds had retained SwissTargetPrediction records, whereas 23 unique structurally tractable ligands entered docking. Docking and single-trajectory molecular dynamics do not determine candidate membership and are not interpreted as direct binding or efficacy evidence.

The separately held simulated-editor v8 candidate package contains 19 files: one manuscript, one cover letter, eight 300-dpi main figures, and nine Additional files. Supplementary Figures S1-S16, Supplementary Tables S1-S10, and Additional files 1-9 remain in strict first-citation order. Final v8 byte sizes and SHA-256 values are frozen in `BMC_PHARMACOLOGY_TOXICOLOGY_ASSET_CHECKSUMS.tsv`; the submission package itself is not copied into GitHub, while the two new machine-readable source workbooks are mirrored under `derived_data/`.

The v8 checksum inventory was frozen after the 3 September 2026 structural and visual review. That review covered all 49 manuscript pages, the one-page cover letter, eight main figures, Supplementary Figs. S1-S16, and 31 workbook previews. Supplementary Fig. S8 was repaired so its colour key no longer crosses CD38/TERT labels, and Supplementary Fig. S13 now carries the a/b panel labels used in its legend.

The v8 interpretation reports five directionally coherent exploratory candidates. Only TOP2A and GSK3B had donor-bootstrap intervals excluding zero; neither remained significant after matched-background multiplicity correction (both BH-adjusted q = 0.317), and the pooled matched-null test was not significant (P = 0.570). The healthy-directed trajectory was retained in all 15 six-month pairs and exceeded both gene-axis and anchor-label permutation nulls. Outcome-blind ranking and one-profile-per-participant WGCNA sensitivities define robustness without changing the prespecified candidate order. scTenifoldKnk response breadth remains descriptive because no eligible candidate exceeded its matched noncandidate distribution after empirical multiplicity correction.

The issued software and derived-data archive remains Zenodo v0.1.0, DOI <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21837457>, corresponding to commit `b850d7e6a6141962d3a2d38021d0780e3c5907a1`. Files and scripts named for prior Human Genomics or Scientific Reports packages are retained as historical regression records only. They are not the current submission definition and must not be used to claim that a nonexistent companion molecular-dynamics DOI was issued.
