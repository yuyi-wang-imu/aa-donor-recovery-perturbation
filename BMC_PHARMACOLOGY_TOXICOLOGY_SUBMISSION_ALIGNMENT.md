# BMC Pharmacology and Toxicology submission alignment

The current manuscript is:

> Donor-aware transcriptomics and bidirectional Geneformer perturbation prioritize compound-associated targets in aplastic anemia

It is prepared as a regular **Research article** for **BMC Pharmacology and Toxicology**. The evidence order is database-compound provenance associated with herbs recurrent in published aplastic-anemia prescriptions, human hematopoietic context, a donor-aware healthy-directed longitudinal transcriptomic trajectory, bidirectional Geneformer prioritization, and downstream network/structural hypothesis generation. The database compounds were not chemically quantified or verified in an administered formulation. Eighteen compounds had retained SwissTargetPrediction records, whereas 23 unique structurally tractable ligands entered docking. Docking and single-trajectory molecular dynamics do not determine candidate membership and are not interpreted as direct binding or efficacy evidence.

The separately held simulated-editor v5 candidate package contains 17 files: one manuscript, one cover letter, eight 300-dpi main figures, and seven Additional files. Supplementary Figures S1-S16, Supplementary Tables S1-S10, and Additional files 1-7 must remain in strict first-citation order. Final v5 byte sizes and SHA-256 values must be regenerated in `BMC_PHARMACOLOGY_TOXICOLOGY_ASSET_CHECKSUMS.tsv` after all edits and visual QA; the submission files themselves are not copied into GitHub.

The v4 checksum inventory was refreshed after the 2 September 2026 typography and supplementary-figure visual-quality review. The v5 revision reopens the checksum boundary: the manuscript, cover letter and any revised supplementary workbook must be rehashed and revalidated before this branch is merged or pushed as the current submission definition.

The v5 interpretation reports five directionally coherent exploratory candidates. Only TOP2A and GSK3B had donor-bootstrap intervals excluding zero; neither remained significant after matched-background multiplicity correction (both BH-adjusted q = 0.317), and the pooled matched-null test was not significant (P = 0.570). scTenifoldKnk response breadth is descriptive because no eligible candidate exceeded its matched noncandidate distribution after empirical multiplicity correction. These negative calibration results are part of the public evidence boundary and must not be omitted from repository documentation.
