# BMC Pharmacology and Toxicology submission alignment

The current manuscript is:

> Donor-aware transcriptomics and bidirectional Geneformer perturbation prioritize compound-associated targets in aplastic anemia

It is prepared as a regular **Research article** for **BMC Pharmacology and Toxicology**. The evidence order is prescription-derived compound provenance, human hematopoietic context, donor-aware longitudinal recovery, bidirectional Geneformer prioritization, and downstream network/structural hypothesis generation. Docking and single-trajectory molecular dynamics do not determine candidate membership and are not interpreted as direct binding or efficacy evidence.

The separately held submission package contains 17 files: one manuscript, one cover letter, eight 300-dpi main figures, and seven Additional files. Supplementary Figures S1-S16, Supplementary Tables S1-S10, and Additional files 1-7 are cited in strict first-citation order. Exact byte sizes and SHA-256 values are recorded in `BMC_PHARMACOLOGY_TOXICOLOGY_ASSET_CHECKSUMS.tsv`; the submission files themselves are not copied into GitHub.

The checksum inventory was refreshed after the final 2 September 2026 typography and supplementary-figure visual-quality review. That review confirmed the manuscript reference sequence (1-103), main-figure sequence (1-8), Supplementary Figure sequence (S1-S16), Supplementary Table sequence (S1-S10), and Additional-file sequence (1-7).

The issued software and derived-data archive remains Zenodo v0.1.0, DOI <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21837457>, corresponding to commit `b850d7e6a6141962d3a2d38021d0780e3c5907a1`. No separate public DOI has been issued for the complete molecular-dynamics production records. The retained six-system production records (five ligand-containing 100-ns trajectories and one matched 100-ns HIF1A-ARNT apo reference; 600 ns total; 13.2 GB) are available on reasonable request from Wendurige (`wendurige@imu.edu.cn`) and will be supplied to editors and reviewers during peer review.

Files and scripts named for prior Human Genomics or Scientific Reports packages are retained as historical regression records only. They are not the current submission definition and must not be used to claim that a nonexistent companion molecular-dynamics DOI was issued.
