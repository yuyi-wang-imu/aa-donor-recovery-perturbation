# Prior graphical-abstract image provenance

This record documents the 920 x 300-pixel graphical abstract prepared for the
prior Human Genomics submission. A visual abstract is optional for the current
BMC Pharmacology and Toxicology Research Article; it has been omitted from the
current submission package and is retained here only for provenance and audit.

The prior image uses the exact manuscript title:

> Donor-Aware Longitudinal Transcriptomics and Bidirectional Geneformer Perturbation Prioritize Recovery-Aligned Genes in Aplastic Anemia

The three medicinal materials are shown with photographic, rather than cartoon,
representations. The Ecliptae Herba and Ligustri Lucidi Fructus source image was
published under CC BY 4.0 in a Frontiers article (DOI:
`10.3389/fphar.2026.1797126`). The Cuscutae Semen crop came from the crude
product shown in Figure 1 of Song Yang et al., *Evidence-Based Complementary
and Alternative Medicine* (2016), DOI: `10.1155/2016/8656740`. That article is
distributed under the Creative Commons Attribution License. The displayed
crops were adjusted only for layout and background consistency.

Medicinal-material identity was checked against the Hong Kong Baptist
University medicinal-material image database. Images from that database were
not copied because the database marks them as all rights reserved.

The prior graphical abstract is a visual summary of the manuscript's evidence
flow. It is not an analytical input and does not imply clinical efficacy,
direct target engagement, or experimental validation of the herb combination.
